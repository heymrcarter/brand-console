# Start here

This design system is for our own teams only. It is not a kit for other companies, and we do not sell it or share it outside the company.

It has two parts that work together: the **Brand Console** and **Storybook**.

## The Brand Console

The Brand Console is where the brand team and the admin team make choices. They pick brand colors, fonts, corners, and settings for each component, like whether radio buttons show as a list or as tiles. The console checks every choice. If words would be hard to read, it stops the change and offers a fix.

When the brand team is happy, they publish. Publishing makes a new version with a number, like 1.5.0. Each version is saved, so the team can always go back.

## Storybook

Storybook shows the real React components that apps use. It always shows the **draft**, which is what the brand team is working on now. Use the toolbar at the top to switch the brand, the screen type, the text size, and light or dark mode.

Each component page shows every kind and state. The **Accessibility** tab at the bottom checks the open story for problems.

## How they work together

1. The brand team changes something in the Brand Console. The console saves it to the token files in `tokens/`.
2. The console rebuilds `build/draft/theme.css` right away. Storybook uses it, so you see the change in every component.
3. The brand team publishes. The console checks everything again, then writes the new version to `dist/` and to `releases/`.
4. Apps get `dist/theme.css`, `dist/settings.json`, and the components. They update when they move to the new version.

## Who does what

- **Brand team:** brand colors, fonts, corners, and component settings. They publish new versions.
- **Admin team:** text and control sizes for each screen type, and rules everyone must follow.
- **Engineers:** the React components, the tests, and the tools. They use the published version in apps.
- **Designers:** Figma. The Figma library syncs with the console both ways.
